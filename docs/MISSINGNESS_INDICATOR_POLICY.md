# Missingness Indicator Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-09

## Decision

Missingness indicators may remain inside predictive models after diagnosis, but they must be separated from economic factor interpretation.

The raw panel preserves null values. The exported dataset does not fill missing accounting values with zeros, repeated prior values, or invented values. Model pipelines may impute internally for training/scoring, and `SimpleImputer(add_indicator=True)` may create missingness flags, but those flags are model-engineering variables rather than SEC accounting facts.

## Total Liabilities Finding

The feature `missingindicator_total_liabilities` is not present in the raw panel. It is generated by the sklearn preprocessing pipeline when `total_liabilities` is missing before median imputation.

The audit found that the old `success_resilience_next_4q` target was contaminated: missing future leverage/liability values could become automatic non-success labels. The panel builder has now been fixed so future success labels remain null when too few future net-income, ROA, and leverage/assets components are observed.

Final post-fix check after the +150 firm expansion, 2026-05-07 period-aligned SEC rebuild, 2026-05-08 alias-collapse rebuild, and 2026-05-09 SEC mapping/caveat-resolution rebuild:

- `success_resilience_next_4q` current known rows: 20,152.
- `success_resilience_next_4q` current unknown rows: 11,550.
- `success_resilience_next_4q` current positive rows among known rows: 12,519.
- Rows currently `0` but revised missing-aware target says unknown: 0.
- Known current/revised target disagreements: 0.

## Interpretation Rule

Use missingness indicators for:

- predictive auxiliary signal;
- reporting-pattern diagnostics;
- parser/source coverage audits.

Do not use missingness indicators as:

- direct economic success factors;
- direct economic failure factors;
- evidence that a firm succeeded or failed because it did or did not report a field.

## Thesis Wording

Safe wording:

> Missingness indicators were retained only as diagnosed predictive auxiliaries. Economic interpretation excludes these indicators and focuses on substantive accounting, ratio, deterioration, sector, macro, and event-context variables.

Unsafe wording:

> Missing total liabilities causes firm success or failure.

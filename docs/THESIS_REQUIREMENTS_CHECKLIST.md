# Thesis Requirements Checklist

Source: `../_external_inputs/01_original_thesis_and_requirements/Final_thesis_requirements.pdf`

## Formatting and General Requirements

- Font size: 14 pt.
- Text alignment: justified.
- First abbreviation use: full term followed by abbreviation in parentheses.
- Borrowed figures/tables require source reference under the title.
- Style must be scientific, logical, literate, and consistent.
- Logical transitions between chapters and sections are explicitly required.
- If initial technical plagiarism check shows more than 20% borrowing, the supervisor must check originality.

## Required Structure

Recommended general structure:

- title page,
- table of contents,
- introduction,
- main chapters and sections,
- conclusion,
- references,
- appendices.

For a master's research-format thesis, the main body should follow a research article structure:

- extended introduction,
- literature review,
- research methodology,
- results,
- discussion of results and future research directions.

## Introduction Must Include

- research problem,
- relevance of the topic,
- degree of prior academic development,
- goal of the work,
- research tasks,
- research object,
- research subject,
- research methods,
- main information sources,
- scientific novelty and/or practical significance,
- structure of the work.

## Empirical and Methodological Requirements

- The thesis must be based on processed empirical material.
- Empirical material should be generalized and structured in appendices where appropriate.
- The work must show ability to use research methods studied in the program.
- The literature must be critically analyzed, not merely summarized.
- The author's own position must be present and linked logically to the topic.
- Results and recommendations must have practical significance and be applicable to organizations or markets.

## Master's Thesis Minimums

- Minimum volume: 70 pages or 95,000 characters with spaces.
- The minimum excludes references and appendices.
- Reference list: at least 50 sources.
- 50-60% of sources should be academic journal articles.
- At least 33% of sources should be foreign English-language academic journal articles.

## Master's Research-Format Evaluation Criteria

- Correct formulation of the research problem and fit with the program profile.
- Structure of the work.
- Logic of presentation.
- Terminological clarity.
- Fit between methods, goal, and tasks.
- Depth of data analysis.
- Correct application of research methods.
- Practical significance and/or scientific novelty.
- Potential for further research.
- Compliance with formatting rules.

## Implications for This Thesis

- The thesis should be written as a research-format VКR, not a project-format company case.
- The core novelty should be explicit: a cross-industry, macro-regime-aware, interpretable ML framework for predicting firm distress and resilience.
- The empirical chapter must describe data collection, cleaning, feature engineering, target construction, validation design, model selection, and limitations.
- The dashboard should be framed as a practical analytical tool that supports the practical significance of the work.
- The appendix should include data dictionary, variable definitions, source tables, additional model metrics, and dashboard/repository notes.

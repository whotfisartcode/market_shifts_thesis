# Exploratory Target Lab

Last updated: 2026-05-10

This note defines the target lab and its promotion boundary. The controlling production facts remain `docs/FINAL_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_INDEX.md` and `docs/FACTUAL_FREEZE_20260509.md`: the production panel is now 31,702 rows x 190 columns, with 540 CIKs and `prediction_date = filed_date`.

The target-lab labels are generated by `scripts/modeling/run_exploratory_target_lab.py`. Four validated secondary labels are now written into the production panel by `scripts/modeling/promote_validated_secondary_targets.py`; diagnostic and robustness-extension labels remain outside the production panel. The four promoted targets must not replace the three primary production targets:

- `distress_next_4q`
- `failure_pressure_conservative_v2_next_4obs`
- `success_resilience_next_4q`

## Shared Rules

- All exploratory targets are forward-looking labels.
- Future accounting observations are used only for target construction, never as model features.
- Post-event rows are excluded from model fitting.
- Strict formal distress in the target window blocks success/resilience/recovery labels.
- Missing future components remain null. They are not converted to zero.
- Rows with fewer than three future observations are generally unknown unless strict formal distress is observed.

## Target Definitions

### `industry_relative_resilience_next_4obs`

Known when at least three future observations have usable sector-year relative profitability and leverage information, or strict formal distress is observed.

Positive when at least three future observations show either ROA or net margin above the relevant sector-year median and leverage/assets is not in the worst sector-year quartile. Strict formal distress overrides the label to zero.

### `stress_resilience_next_4obs`

Evaluated only when market stress is elevated at the current prediction date using `stress_elevated_v2`.

Positive when the firm remains healthy in at least three of the next four future filing observations. Healthy means positive net income, positive ROA, and leverage/assets between 0 and 0.85. Non-stress rows remain null, not zero.

### `recovery_next_4obs`

Evaluated only for currently weak firms. Current weakness is based on current accounting state: non-positive net income, non-positive ROA, leverage/assets above 0.85, current ratio below 1.0, or cash/assets below 0.03.

Positive when at least two future observations become healthy, with at least two future observations showing positive net income, positive ROA, and acceptable leverage/assets. Strict formal distress overrides recovery to zero.

### `deterioration_next_4obs`

Evaluated for firms not already in serious current pressure. Serious current pressure means non-positive net income, non-positive ROA, or leverage/assets above 0.85.

Positive when the future window shows formal distress, production failure-pressure, repeated negative profitability, material leverage increase, or material equity/assets decline. This target is related to, but broader than, the production failure-pressure label.

### `quality_success_cashflow_next_4obs`

Known when at least three future observations have usable net income, ROA, leverage/assets, and operating cash flow, or strict formal distress is observed.

Positive when at least three future observations show positive net income, positive ROA, positive operating cash flow, acceptable leverage/assets, and no strict formal distress.

### `sector_relative_improvement_next_4obs`

Robustness-extension target. Evaluated only for firms that start below their sector-year profitability median. A firm is currently below peer median when the available current profitability measures, ROA and/or net margin, are not above the sector-year median.

Known when the row is applicable, at least three future observations have usable sector-year relative profitability information, or strict formal distress is observed. Positive when the firm moves above the sector-year median on ROA or net margin in at least two of the next four observations and no strict formal distress occurs in the four-quarter target window.

### `persistent_resilience_next_6obs`

Robustness-extension target. Known when at least five of the next six future observations have usable net income, ROA, and leverage/assets information, or a strict formal distress event is observed in the six-quarter legal-distress window.

Positive when at least five of the next six future observations are healthy. Healthy means positive net income, positive ROA, and leverage/assets between 0 and 0.85. Strict formal distress in the six-quarter window overrides the label to zero.

## Current Model Sanity

Current outputs are in `reports/target_lab/exploratory_model_metrics.csv`.

The first run suggested four target-lab labels were empirically usable as validated secondary outcomes: industry-relative resilience, stress resilience, recovery, and cash-flow-supported success. After calibration, ranking, interpretation, and promotion audits, these four are now validated secondary production target columns. `deterioration_next_4obs` is learnable but materially weaker and should be treated as a warning/diagnostic target rather than a headline result.

The robustness-extension run adds two creative labels. `persistent_resilience_next_6obs` is strong enough for long-horizon sensitivity analysis. `sector_relative_improvement_next_4obs` is scientifically useful because it captures dynamic peer catch-up, but it is materially weaker than the validated secondary outcomes and should remain an exploratory robustness target.

## Robustness Extension Target Audit

Audit outputs:

- `reports/target_lab/robustness_extension_target_audit.csv`
- `reports/target_lab/robustness_extension_target_audit_summary.md`

The audit passes with zero blocking invariant violations. It checks that the two robustness-extension targets are not production-panel columns, do not enter the model feature list, are missing-aware, are not assigned on post-event rows, and respect strict-distress overrides.

Scientific caveats from the audit:

- `sector_relative_improvement_next_4obs` is an OR-based ROA/net-margin peer catch-up label rather than a composite-index target;
- 68 known sector-relative improvement rows use sector-year peer groups with fewer than 10 rows, and 86 use groups with fewer than 20 rows;
- `persistent_resilience_next_6obs` has 94.9% agreement and 0.896 correlation with `success_resilience_next_4q`, so its value is durability confirmation rather than an independent primary dimension.

## Secondary Analytical Outcome Decision

After calibration, ranking, interpretation checks, and production-promotion audit, four target-lab labels are validated secondary production outcomes:

| Target | Validation status | Main caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `industry_relative_resilience_next_4obs` | Validated secondary production outcome | Sector-year relative thresholds must be described as label-construction logic, not causal peer effects. |
| `stress_resilience_next_4obs` | Validated secondary production outcome | Stress-gated target; high positive base rate makes ranking lift modest despite strong PR-AUC. |
| `recovery_next_4obs` | Validated secondary production outcome | Feature evidence is strongly leverage-sensitive because current weakness and future health definitions include leverage. |
| `quality_success_cashflow_next_4obs` | Validated secondary production outcome | Cash-flow quality is visible but leverage/profitability remain stronger model drivers. |
| `deterioration_next_4obs` | Diagnostic only | Weaker first-pass performance and rarer positives. |
| `sector_relative_improvement_next_4obs` | Robustness extension | Moderate calibrated test PR-AUC and useful top-rank lift, but not strong enough to promote. |
| `persistent_resilience_next_6obs` | Robustness extension | Strong long-horizon sensitivity outcome, but lower known-row count and longer-horizon selection effects mean it should not replace `success_resilience_next_4q`. |

The first four are production target-label columns and support dimensional thesis analysis and dashboard Target Lab views. The two robustness-extension targets support sensitivity and discussion, not headline claims.
